About InAmigos Foundation

Building India,
One Community
at a Time

Founded on September 23, 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh — InAmigos Foundation (IAF) is a government-registered NGO committed to creating an inclusive, empowered, and sustainable India.

With a network of 200+ volunteers across 28 states and 6 impactful projects, IAF works tirelessly to bridge gaps in education, healthcare, environment, and economic opportunities.

Mission: "To empower communities through compassion, education, sustainability, and collective action."

Vision: "A world where every individual has equal opportunities, dignity, education, and hope."

FAQs

Common Questions

What is InAmigos Foundation?
▼
InAmigos Foundation (IAF) is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization founded in 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh. It is ISO 9001:2015 certified, 80G & 12A certified, CSR-1 registered, and recognized by NITI Aayog (NGO Darpan). IAF runs 6 impactful projects across education, environment, animal welfare, women empowerment, food distribution, and youth skill development.
How can I volunteer with IAF?
▼
You can volunteer by filling the contact form on this page or emailing support@inamigosfoundation.org.in. IAF has volunteer opportunities across 28 states in India in areas ranging from field work, digital operations, content writing, research, and more. You can also apply for an internship through Project VIKAS.
How can I donate to InAmigos Foundation?
▼
InAmigos Foundation accepts donations for all its 6 projects. Donations are eligible for 80G tax exemption. Contact support@inamigosfoundation.org.in or call +91 6267 309 902 for donation details. Your contribution directly funds food kits, tree plantations, education materials, and more.
What is Project VIKAS and how do I apply?
▼
Project VIKAS is IAF's internship & youth skill development initiative. It has trained 30,000+ interns across India in fields like research, finance, marketing, content writing, HR, and digital operations. Apply by emailing Hr@inamigosfoundation.org.in or through IAF's official website at inamigosfoundation.org.in.
Is IAF active in my city/state?
▼
Yes! InAmigos Foundation currently operates across 28 states in India. From Chhattisgarh to Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, Rohtak, and beyond — IAF's volunteer network reaches communities nationwide. Contact the foundation to find a chapter near you.
Can companies partner with IAF for CSR?
▼
Absolutely. InAmigos Foundation is CSR-1 registered, enabling seamless corporate partnerships for impactful CSR initiatives across all 6 project areas. Email support@inamigosfoundation.org.in to discuss partnership opportunities aligned with your company's CSR goals.
